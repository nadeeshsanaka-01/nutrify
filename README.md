# Nutrify Web Application
A growing body of research proves that fruits are critical to promoting good health.
In fact, fruits and vegetables should be the foundation of a healthy diet. Most
people need to double the amount of fruits and vegetables they eat every day.
Fruits supply dietary fibre and fibre intake is linked to lower incidence of
cardiovascular disease and obesity. Fruits and vegetables also supply vitamins and
minerals to the diet and are sources of phytochemicals that function as
antioxidants, phytoestrogens, and antiinflammatory agents and through other
protective mechanisms.
In this scenario, it is not only important to consume the right amount of fruits but
also it is important to the nutritional value of fruits we consume.
The project ‘Nutrify Web Application’ is a web-based application that gives a user
the nutritional information of fruit when fruit name is entered.
